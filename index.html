Pause & Wonder | RZ.01

Kitchen/Table
2026

RZ.01 is knife made for bakers. I designed it because no other knife checked all of my boxes for a perfect  baking-specific cutting tool. My large knifes are hard to sharpen and awkward to use on small pastries, and razor blades are easy to loose and and hard to keep track of how sharp they are. RZ.01 is a a tool refined for the craft of pastry, yet versatile for everyday use. It is also the first product I'm selling on my personal product brand Pause & Wonder.

‍

Available at https://pausewonder.com/

Janeiro

Client | Furniture
2022

Janeiro is designed to foster the creation and nurturance of personal relationships in a highly mobile work environment. With a warm and approachable aesthetic, Janeiro invites people to lounge and talk, creating fleeting moments of casual conversation that cultivate and strengthen human connection. Inspired by the harmonious interchange between mountain and sea that is emblematic of the city of Rio de Janeiro, Janeiro's clean design and elegant details mirror this city's inviting landscape. Its small footprint and enhanced mobility, driven by its small wheels, render Janeiro a highly versatile chair equally fit for small and large social spaces.
