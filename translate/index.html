Chinese in the air, English in your ears.

Tap Start, then hold your phone toward whoever's speaking. Each finished sentence gets read into your AirPods.

Two-way also turns your English into Chinese you can show them. Listen only translates Chinese into English.

First, add your Soniox API key in Settings.

Settings

Stored only on this device. Get one at console.soniox.com.
Pick "iPhone Microphone" if it's listed: it hears other people much better than the AirPods mic. Tap Start once to unlock this list.
For better voices, download an Enhanced or Premium one in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English.
Off by default because the audio plays in your AirPods. Use the "Show" button on a reply to show the screen instead.
If several sentences pile up, drop the oldest so the voice stays near real time.
